# Cultural briefing: TPOT, Visakan Veerasamy, and the thread-web genre

> Research compiled by a Claude agent, 2026-07-06, from full-text fetches of primary sources (visakanv.com, aaronzlewis.com, thenewatlantis.com, vibe.camp, xiqo.substack.com, GitHub, Wayback Machine). Claims from prior model knowledge are marked "(unverified)".

## 1. TPOT — "This Part of Twitter"

**What it is.** A loose Twitter/X subculture of ex-rationalists, "postrats," and adjacent curious weirdos. Tara Isabella Burton's ["Rational Magic"](https://www.thenewatlantis.com/publications/rational-magic) (*The New Atlantis*, Spring 2023) defines it: "a loose online subculture known as the postrationalists — also known by the jokey endonym 'this part of Twitter,' or TPOT... writers, thinkers, readers, and Internet trolls alike who were once rationalists, or members of adjacent communities like the effective altruism movement, but grew disillusioned." The name is deliberately indexical/anti-definitional. Earlier endonyms: **"the ingroup"** and **"TCoT (this corner of Twitter)"**.

**Lineage.** Rationalism: Overcoming Bias (2006) → LessWrong (2009) → Slate Star Codex/Astral Codex Ten → CFAR, EA, x-risk. The postrationalist reaction kept the discourse norms (charity, debate ideas not people, truth-seeking) but rejected hyper-utilitarian legibility, adding — per [vibe.camp/history](https://vibe.camp/history) — "earnestness & sincerity as a pushback against rampant irony-poisoning, plus an interest in illegibility and nontraditional psychological, therapeutic and contemplative practices." Postrat canon bloggers: **Venkatesh Rao** (Ribbonfarm), **Sarah Perry** ("It is better to be interesting and wrong than it is to be right and boring"), **David Chapman** (Meaningness). Related umbrella terms: **"the metatribe"** (Tyler Alterman, Sept 2020), **"the liminal web"** (Joe Lightfoot, 2021), "the sensemaking web."

**Timeline & peak.** Proto-scene of rationalists shitposting through the late 2010s; the pandemic (2020) "sent thousands of suddenly-stuck-at-home nerds to Twitter, seeking connection" (vibe.camp/history). The name "tpot" solidified ~2021. **Vibecamp** is the canonical institutionalization — Brooke Bowman (@gptbrooke); the site calls its origin a **"hyperstition"**; Vibecamp 1 ran March 2022 outside Austin; now annual at Camp Ramblewood, MD (~500 people). Rough peak: **2020–2022**; the Musk acquisition (Oct 2022) and API shutdown degraded the substrate, with partial diaspora to Bluesky/Substack 2023–24. IRL spinoffs: **Office Hours** (deepfates), **Fractal NYC**, and Vibecamp "partially inspired **Manifest**, ... itself upstream of **LessOnline**."

**Norms & aesthetics.** Earnestness-as-rebellion; "vibes" as first-class epistemic object; **mutuals** ("moots"); reply-guy culture reframed as a virtue (Visa: "I am openly happy to describe myself as a reply guy. The difference is that I'm good at it"); generous quote-tweeting of old tweets as conversation currency; pseudonymous micro-celebrities blending "sincerity and shitposting" (Burton); woo-friendly practices — meditation/jhanas, tarot, circling, Alexander Technique. Tim Blais' Nov 2021 "IN THIS HOUSE WE" meme, quoted on vibe.camp/history: "REPLY like it's IMPROV / hold our beliefs LOOSELY / be FRIENDLY, AMBITIOUS NERDS / FLIRT with ABANDON / MUTE POLITICAL RETWEETS / AMPLIFY the GOOD / DISTRUST UNHAPPY INTELLIGENCE / and always ALWAYS like before replying."

**Key figures.** @visakanv (§2); **eigenrobot**; **Aella**; **QC/Qiaochu Yuan** (unverified detail); **nosilverv**; **deepfates**; **Nick Cammarata** (unverified); **Tyler Alterman**; **Brooke Bowman**; **Tasshin Fogleman**; **xiq/@exgenesis** (Community Archive); **Prince Vogelfrei**; **Katherine Dee/@default_friend**; **Zero HP Lovecraft** (read by postrats, rejects the label); **Peter Limberg** (The Stoa); **John Vervaeke**.

## 2. Visakan Veerasamy (@visakanv)

**Background.** Singaporean, b. ~1990. Tamil; flunked out of Singapore's Gifted Education Program; music-scene kid; first marketing hire at **ReferralCandy**; co-founded **Statement.sg**. Now an independent writer (Patreon/Substack/books). Main site: [visakanv.com](https://visakanv.com/).

**Major projects.**
- **@1000wordvomits** ([visakanv.com/1000/](http://www.visakanv.com/1000/)): "1,000 stream-of-consciousness essays of 1,000 words each" — 821/1000 as of Jan 2023.
- **Friendly Ambitious Nerd** (2020) — "Marcus Aurelius's Meditations for Twitter Addicts," assembled substantially from tweets; **INTROSPECT** (2021).
- **do 100 things** ([blog](https://www.visakanv.com/blog/do100things/), 2021): "The magic of 'do 100 things' is that there's nothing else in that sentence." Companions: "be so prolific you don't recognize yourself," "quantity leads to quality."
- **Scenius**: [the death and life of great scenius](https://www.visakanv.com/blog/scenius/) — golden ages come from "a bunch of nerds trying to outdo each other."
- ~155k+ tweets; thread index at [visakanv.com/blog/threads/](https://www.visakanv.com/blog/threads/) and a public Notion catalog.

**The threading methodology — structural mechanics** (all self-documented):

- **Reply-to-self chains as accretion.** Threads are never "done"; appended across years. From [🧵hyperthreading](https://www.visakanv.com/blog/hyperthreading/): "Hyperthreading is not threading + threading, but rather, threading **×** threading. It's multiplicative." Also: "my hyperthreading is an ADHD coping mechanism."
- **Quote-tweets as hyperlinks.** From [twitter_rpg_strategy_guide.txt](https://www.visakanv.com/blog/twitter/) (2019): "Any tweet from any thread can be reused in any other thread,,, do you guys realise how wild that is??? It allows you to create your own cinematic universe."
- **Search as the retrieval layer.** "I'm searching my own tweets all the time... you'll write things anticipating the searches of future-you."
- **Threads as trailheads; revival over archiving.** "broke: viral tweet / woke: tweet that's referenced & reused in many threads." Ideas "have to be in play as currency, not frozen into plaques in museums."
- **Threading as a solution to fragmentation** (Apr 2018, status/986554117790228480): "What would be ideal is if all information could be represented as 'cards', and all cards could be easily threaded." Coinages: "assemble the **mindcity**," "**desire paths**," "thread of threads of threads."
- **The self-QT as review/annotation.** In [my creative process](https://www.visakanv.com/blog/my-creative-process/): a 10-step anatomy of a representative thread; "working on essays produces tweets and working on tweets produces essays"; the no-edit constraint — "you can subsequently recontextualize it by quoting it in a new thread... but you can't edit what you've already written."
- **Origin artifact:** Kahlil Corazo (RoamBrain, 2020) points to tweet 718829479921987584 (April 2016) as the moment Visa discovered self-quote interlinking; calls the corpus "a work of art. We see the history of his craft in the artwork itself."
- Audience filter: "I tweet for 0.1% outliers in optimism, thoughtfulness, creativity, kindness, competence, ambition, drive, curiosity."

## 3. The genre — networked public thinking, off-Twitter attempts

**Distinctive properties:** additive/immutable units composed into growing sequences; associative rather than hierarchical organization; the QT as first-class citation primitive; search-first retrieval; audience-in-the-loop; longitudinal — a thread is a **live essay** recording its own revision history. Visa's mantra: "**thinking is easy, information architecture is hard**" ([post](https://www.visakanv.com/blog/information-architecture/)) — where he also warns: "For some period of time I was painfully fixated on trying to represent my entire twitter corpus, I even unintentionally sent a couple of earnest devs down that hellhole, none have returned lol... You can't eat the sun."

**Archive/export attempts:**
- Visa's own: threads index post, Notion catalog, Google Doc of every 100+-fav tweet, public Roam graph (roamresearch.com/#/app/visakanv-public), [visakanv.com/archives](https://www.visakanv.com/archives/), and the two books.
- **ThreadHelper** (2021, xiq/@exgenesis): "A serendipity engine on the Twitter sidebar" — surfaced your own relevant old tweets while typing; dormant post-API-lockdown.
- **The Community Archive** ([community-archive.org](https://www.community-archive.org/), Sept 2024, xiq): "a public database and API of the full twitter history of everyone who uploads their archive... a backup of the twitter canon." Aims: semantic search, "producing books from tweet collections," "LLM-based digital anthropology," "tracing the diffusion of ideas over time," Bluesky migration. Satellites: **birdseye** (per-account topic clustering), **ca-top-qts** ("tpot canon"), **memetic-lineage**.
- Genre-adjacent: Venkatesh Rao's **blogchains**, Epsilon Theory's **Discovery Map**, **Are.na** channels, Thread Reader unrolls.

## 4. Writing about the phenomenon

- **Aaron Z. Lewis, ["The spreading of threading"](https://aaronzlewis.com/blog/2019/05/01/spreading-threading/)** (May 2019) — the key analysis. "Visa's threads are doing to Twitter what hyperlinks did to dead-tree text... Without thread-webs, most tweets get left behind in the past." Frames the practice as "Resurrecting the Memex." "He's constructing a digital version of his brain, and his curation style feels like an early iteration of something that might one day go mainstream."
- **Daniel McNichol, ["Ingroup Colossus: @visakanv"](https://medium.com/coemeta/ingroup-colossus-visakanv-744c08f408f2)** (Jan 2022) — data-science analysis of the "organic big account playbook"; "a lavish longitudinal autoethnography."
- **Tara Isabella Burton, ["Rational Magic"](https://www.thenewatlantis.com/publications/rational-magic)** (Spring 2023) — definitive mainstream account of TPOT/postrationalism.
- **vibe.camp/history** and **vibe.camp/media** — insider periodization + scene retrospectives.
- **Kahlil Corazo** (RoamBrain, 2020) — the zettelkasten world's reading of Visa's tweet graph.
