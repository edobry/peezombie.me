# Design Language & UX Patterns

This document defines the visual and experiential principles that guide the look and feel of the site, rooted in minimalism, semantic clarity, and a computational/biomimicral aesthetic.

## Visual Theme

- Default dark mode.
- Mostly monochrome palette with subtle, muted accent colors.
- Emphasis on contrast, clarity, and atmosphere.

## Aesthetic Motifs

- Visual elements and micro-interactions inspired by natural computation (e.g. ECA-like patterns, cellular behavior, organic emergence).
- Backgrounds may feature subtle animated patterns or textures.

## Typography

- Harmonious and intentional typographic hierarchy.
- Should express personality while remaining legible and grounded.
- Carefully chosen type scales and font pairings.

## Layout & Responsiveness

- Modular layout system that is:
  - Structured and intentional in design.
  - Reconfigurable for responsiveness across screen sizes.
- Components and sections are composable, allowing content to rearrange fluidly.

## Experience Design

- Follows principles of clear information architecture and wayfinding.
- Uses progressive disclosure to reveal complexity only when needed.
- Prioritizes focus, legibility, and semantic grouping of elements.
