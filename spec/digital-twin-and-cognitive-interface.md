# Digital Twin & Cognitive Interface

This document outlines the long-term vision for the site as an interactive digital twin of your Self, informed by your data, voice, and semantics. It also explores the relationship between the tag system, exocortex, and conversational interface.

## Core Concept

- The site is designed as a computational reflection of your Self.
- It is not just a portfolio or CMS, but a persistent cognitive interface for others to interact with your thinking, knowledge, and creative output.

## Chatbot Interface

- Visitors can engage with a chatbot designed to reflect your tone, voice, and personality.
- The chatbot’s knowledge is constrained by the visitor’s access level (based on their identity or auth status).
- It uses the same semantic tag system to contextualize responses.

## Tag System Integration

- The tag structure provides grounding and retrieval capabilities for the chatbot.
- Tags function as nodes in a dynamic knowledge graph, informing what the chatbot can "see" and how it frames answers.

## Exocortical Symbiosis

- The digital twin functions in concert with your exocortex.
- Tags help scaffold memory retrieval, semantic navigation, and personal reasoning pathways.
- This layer of interaction informs both how you navigate your own archive and how others interact with your ideas.
